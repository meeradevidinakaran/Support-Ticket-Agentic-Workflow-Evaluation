🎫 8 Sample Tickets
Use Subject / body as user prompts.
Detailed Examples (good classification candidates)
	1. Billing Error
		○ Subject: “Charged twice for July subscription”
		○ Body: “I was billed two times for my July SaaS subscription. My invoice IDs are #78432 and #78433. Please confirm and refund the duplicate charge.”
		○ Category: Billing
		○ Urgency: High (money involved)
		○ Actionability: Clear, with evidence.
	2. Technical Bug
		○ Subject: “Dashboard not loading after update”
		○ Body: “Since yesterday’s update, my analytics dashboard shows a blank screen. I tried clearing cache and logging out/in, but the issue persists.”
		○ Category: Technical Issue
		○ Urgency: Medium
		○ Actionability: Detailed reproduction steps.
	3. Account Access
		○ Subject: “Password reset link expired”
		○ Body: “I requested a password reset, but the link expired within minutes. Can you send me a new one?”
		○ Category: Account Access
		○ Urgency: Medium
		○ Actionability: Specific request.
	4. Feature Request
		○ Subject: “Need export to CSV option”
		○ Body: “Our team needs to export reports to CSV for compliance. Currently, only PDF is available. Can you add CSV export?”
		○ Category: Feature Request
		○ Urgency: Low
		○ Actionability: Clear enhancement request.
Vague Examples (hallucination risk)
I'm so frustrated!!! I've been trying to do something for hours and NOTHING is working. This is ridiculous. I want my money back NOW.
	5. Unclear Complaint
		○ Subject: “Nothing works, fix it”
		○ Body: “Your app is broken. Please fix ASAP.”
		○ Category: Unknown
		○ Urgency: Needs clarification
		○ Actionability: Too vague — AI should ask for details.
	6. Minimal Input
		○ Subject: “Help”
		○ Body: “Help.”
		○ Category: Unknown
		○ Urgency: Needs clarification
		○ Actionability: No context — AI must not invent.
	7. Generic Frustration
		○ Subject: “Terrible service”
		○ Body: “I’m really unhappy with your product.”
		○ Category: Unknown
		○ Urgency: Needs clarification
		○ Actionability: Emotional but no specifics.
	8. Ambiguous Request
		○ Subject: “Problem with account”
		○ Body: “My account isn’t working right.”
		○ Category: Unknown
		○ Urgency: Needs clarification
		○ Actionability: Ambiguous — could be billing, login, or technical.
